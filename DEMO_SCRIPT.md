# LessonLens Demo Script

## 60-Second Pitch

LessonLens helps teachers get useful classroom feedback from a short transcript. Many teachers, especially in government school contexts, do not get frequent coaching after a lesson. When they do, it can feel delayed or evaluative.

With LessonLens, a teacher can paste a classroom moment and receive a practical coaching card in seconds. The card focuses on student participation, question quality, language clarity and pacing, one action to try tomorrow, one strength to keep, and a warm encouragement message.

The goal is not grading the teacher. The goal is one small, specific improvement for the next class.

## 2-Minute Demo

Start with the `Interactive class` sample.

1. Say: "I will start with a classroom transcript where the teacher is already using discussion and follow-up questions."
2. Select `Interactive class` from the sample dropdown.
3. Click `Load Sample Transcript`.
4. Say: "This fills the transcript box with a realistic classroom exchange."
5. Click `Analyze My Class`.
6. While loading, say: "LessonLens is looking for participation patterns, question quality, clarity, pacing, and one practical next move."
7. When feedback appears, point to the Classroom Snapshot first.
8. Say: "The feedback is designed to be specific and non-judgmental. It uses evidence from the transcript instead of generic advice."
9. Point to the three metric cards.
10. Say: "Here we can see participation, question quality, and pacing separated into teacher-friendly coaching notes."
11. Point to Try This Tomorrow.
12. Say: "The most important part is this: one action the teacher can use tomorrow, including a sample phrase."
13. Click `Start Over` or `Clear`.
14. Select `Lecture-heavy class`.
15. Say: "Now I can switch to a teacher-dominated transcript, and the feedback should change clearly toward low participation and more recall-heavy questioning."

## First Sample To Use

Use `Interactive class` first. It shows the best version of the coaching card and makes the product feel encouraging before showing contrast with the lecture-heavy sample.

## What To Say When Feedback Appears

"Notice that LessonLens does not score or shame the teacher. It gives a clear snapshot, evidence from the classroom transcript, and one realistic next step. The teacher leaves with something they can say in class tomorrow, without needing extra materials."
